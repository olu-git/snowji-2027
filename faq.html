FAQ

Do I need to know how to ski or snowboard?

No. SNOWJI is for first-timers through to pros. If you're new, add the A$99 beginner lesson to your Snow Pass.

What if I'm not into snow sports?

Get the Vibe Pass: six nights' accommodation, the group dinner and two activities in Queenstown, without the ski days.

What's on the mountain with the Snow Pass?

Three ski days plus one night skiing session at Coronet Peak, with lift passes, rental gear and mountain transport.

Are flights included?

No. Return flights from Melbourne with Air New Zealand are an optional add-on (A$1,099, with a separate A$99 deposit). It includes a 23 kg checked bag, 7 kg carry-on and return airport coach transfers in Queenstown. You can book your own flights, but airport transfers only come with our flight add-on.

What does the rental gear include?

Skiing: skis, ski boots, poles and a helmet. Snowboarding: a snowboard with bindings, snowboard boots and a helmet. Snow jackets, pants, gloves and goggles aren't included.

How does the payment plan work?

Pay the A$99 deposit to lock in your spot. Instalments start in January 2027, or you can pay in full at any time. Pay by BECS direct debit to avoid extra transaction fees. Booking fees apply.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable, including if there's no snow or the lifts aren't running. Once you've chosen a package, it can't be changed. We strongly recommend travel insurance.

I'm a uni student. Is there a discount?

Let us know you're an Australian or New Zealand university student at checkout. If a discount is available, we'll email you the details.

Can I room with my friends?

You can list up to three preferred roommates at checkout. We'll do our best, but requests can't be guaranteed.

Do I need a passport?

Yes, a valid passport. If you're booking our flights, make sure the name you give us matches your passport.

Who can come?

SNOWJI is 18+. Wheelchair-accessible options are available, so get in touch.

Still got questions?
